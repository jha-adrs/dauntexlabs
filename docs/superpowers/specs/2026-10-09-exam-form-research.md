# India exam & application-form tools: research (2026-10-09)

This is read-only research for a planned set of exam and form upload tools on dauntexlabs. "Official" means I read the text directly from the issuer's PDF or page. "Official (search extract)" means the text came from an issuer-domain document as quoted by search, but I could not download it myself. "Unconfirmed" means only secondary sources (coaching sites, resizer sites) carry it. Those sources are often a cycle out of date.

## 4. Technical notes: validation and rejections

- **What portals check.** All portals enforce **file size (KB min and max)** and **JPG/JPEG** at upload. Pixel dimensions are usually "preferred" (IBPS/SBI) or "about" in cm (SSC, CTET, TNPSC). Some enforce hard pixel bounds: UPSC 350–500 px for the signature; Indian visa 350–1000 px and square, stated as mandatory. RRB sets a minimum of 140×60. DPI is advisory scanning guidance (200 dpi for IBPS/TNPSC/PAN, ≥100 for RRB). Portals cannot meaningfully check it, but tools should still write correct JFIF density.
- **Live capture is taking over photo uploads** (SSC OTR, RRB CEN 09/2025, UPSC, CUET, IBPS). UPSC face-matches the live capture against the uploaded photo. For SSC and RRB, the photo tool matters less than the **signature** tool.
- **Minimums matter.** Many limits have a floor (UPSC 20 KB, RRB 30 KB, NTA 10 KB, PDFs 50 KB). A tiny clean signature can fall *under* the minimum, which is why people search "increase image size in kb". Tools must be able to pad up (raise quality or dimensions) as well as compress down.
- **KB ambiguity.** Notices never say whether 1 KB is 1000 or 1024 bytes. Inference: target the middle of the range, not the edges.
- **Common rejection grounds** (RRB CEN 09/2025 lists them; SSC/IBPS/SBI say the same):
  - non-white background
  - non-black ink
  - signature in BLOCK/CAPITAL letters
  - blurred, miniature or unclear image
  - partly visible signature
  - wrong image (e.g. the photo in the signature slot)
  - thumb impression in place of a signature
  - a photo of an existing photo (SSC)
  - cap or spectacles (SSC)
  - smudged thumb or declaration (IBPS)
  - photo older than the allowed window, or missing name/date (TNPSC)
  - live photo that doesn't match the uploaded one (UPSC)
